What it will cover

Scope: graph algorithms.

Nothing here is released. This is the scope of the plan, as PLAN.md states it: item 16 of wave 3 (go-graphalgo: matching, isomorphism, communities, the centralities gonum lacks; gonum/graph already has max flow and betweenness, so they are reused and not rewritten).

⇄

Matching

Hopcroft-Karp for bipartite graphs and Edmonds' blossom algorithm for general graphs: gonum has neither.

≅

Isomorphism

VF2 for matching subgraphs and a canonical labelling after nauty for canonical forms.

✂

Cuts and flows

Minimum-cut extraction from gonum's Dinic maximum flow, and push-relabel only if it is needed.

★

Centralities

The ones gonum lacks; Brandes betweenness, PageRank and HITS are already there and are reused.

🧩

A MATLAB-style facade

graph, digraph, adjacency, incidence and Laplacian matrices over gonum/graph, which already covers the structures.

⚖️

Licences kept clean

NetworkX (BSD-3-Clause) is the oracle; the igraph C core (GPL-2.0) is an oracle only.
